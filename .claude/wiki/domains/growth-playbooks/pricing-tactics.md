# Pricing Tactics

Pricing insights from founders who've optimized for maximum revenue. These are not theories — they're validated through real revenue data.

---

## 1. Raise Prices Iteratively (Poppy AI)

**Method**: After every 5-7 sales, increase price by $25. Watch sales velocity. When velocity drops significantly, reduce price slightly. Hold where velocity recovers.

**Result**: Found the optimal price that maximizes both profitability AND volume.

**Why it works**: "I let the market decide. I want to price at the intersection of max profitability and max liquidity."

**Simple framework**:
- $0 price = massive liquidity (tons of users), zero profit
- Max price = max per-unit profit, but nobody buys
- Sweet spot = right in the middle, found empirically

---

## 2. Remove Monthly, Keep Annual Only (Poppy AI)

**Result**: Revenue 3x'd immediately.

**Why it works**:
- Annual customers are more committed and stay longer
- Refund rates dropped
- No failed payment follow-ups (monthly = 12 chances to fail per year; annual = 1)
- No need for a customer support team chasing failed payments
- No tire-kickers testing for one month and leaving

**Key insight**: "I didn't have to build out an entire operations team to follow up on monthly payments."

---

## 3. Add a Lifetime Tier Above Annual (Poppy AI)

**Method**: Instead of monthly ($40) + annual ($400), switched to annual ($400) + lifetime ($1,200).

**Result**: Same number of total buyers as before, but 10-20% chose lifetime = extra $20-40K/month.

**Why it works**: People need two options. The specific options matter less than having a choice. The lifetime tier serves as a price anchor that makes annual feel reasonable, AND some people actually buy it.

**Math**: Lifetime at $1,200 = 3 years of annual. After 3 years, the customer is "profitable." This is a bet on your own retention.

---

## 4. Lifetime Deals as Launch Capital (Mike's Playbook)

**Three-phase LTD strategy**:
1. **Private LTD** ($59-100): Sell in Reddit, Facebook groups, LTD communities → ~$30K
2. **AppSumo LTD**: Leverage their massive email list → additional capital
3. **Final private LTD**: Higher prices, FOMO messaging, close forever

**Goal**: $100K in the bank from LTDs to fund 1-2 years of content and growth before MRR takes over.

**Rules**:
- Never give away free accounts — if people pay, they use it
- LTD customers give you feedback, write reviews, become ambassadors
- Close LTD forever after the final round — creates urgency and protects MRR

---

## 5. Low Price + High Volume + Emotional Pain (Eugene)

**Method**: $5-20/mo price point solving deeply emotional problems (checking if partner is on dating apps, who viewed your Instagram).

**Result**: $40K/mo and $30K/mo respectively.

**Why it works**: The price is low enough that anyone will pay. The pain is so real that demand is massive. Zero competition because nobody thinks to build these.

---

## 6. High-Ticket Upsells via Onboarding (Poppy AI)

**Method**: Use free 1-on-1 onboarding calls (500/month) as a sales channel. Offer a $5K-$15K "done with you" package.

**Result**: Building to $500K/mo additional from 20% conversion rate.

**Key insight**: One customer said he would've paid $20K without flinching. Founders chronically underprice high-ticket offers. Customers doing $400K/mo revenue see $5-15K as a no-brainer compared to agencies ($50-100K/year).

## Pricing Principles Summary

| Principle | Evidence |
|-----------|----------|
| Let the market set the price | Poppy raised incrementally until velocity dropped |
| Fewer payment options = more revenue | Removing monthly 3x'd Poppy's revenue |
| Price anchoring works | Lifetime tier made annual feel cheap |
| Never give away free | Mike: "If people pay, they use it. That's key." |
| LTDs are launch capital, not a business model | Mike closes LTDs forever after raising ~$100K |
| Charge more than you think | Poppy's customer said $20K wouldn't have been a stretch |
| Emotional pain commands premium | Eugene's micro-SaaS proves people pay for relief, not features |

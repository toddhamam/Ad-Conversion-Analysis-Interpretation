# Cold Email Strategy

The core framework for cold email outreach, derived from research and experimentation (March 2026) [source: raw/cold-email-resources.md].

## PWOC Framework

**P**ersonalization → **P**roof → **O**ffer → **C**TA

```
CVR = (ROI × Trust) / Friction
```

- Quantify the ROI
- Add trust via social proof (even brief — never skip it)
- Minimize friction in CTA
- **80% about THEM, 20% about you** — most bad emails are inverted

## Subject Line Principles

- **Plausible deniability beats pain-point subjects** — should look like it could be from someone they know
- Lowercase, 2-5 words, no marketing language, no punctuation
- High-performing: "quick question", "hey {first_name}", "thought of you"
- Pain-point subjects get decent opens (82%) but prime the reader to be defensive

## Copy Rules

- No em dashes (AI giveaway)
- 50-100 words ideal body copy, 150 max
- One CTA only, lowest possible friction
- Write like you'd text a professional acquaintance
- Casualize company names (strip Inc/LLC/Ltd suffixes)
- No links, no images, no HTML in first email
- End with: "Reply STOP to opt out."

## What Works

- Two-touch sequence: follow-up 1 boosts replies by 49%
- Under 80 words body copy
- Casual tone, plain text
- Personalization hooks referencing specific company activity
- Mixed subject line pool (pain-point + plausible deniability) for natural A/B testing
